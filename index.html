Paste shops from Google Maps

  1. Tap Find shops on Google Maps — it opens Google Maps already searching your area.
  2. Long-press a shop's pin (or right-click on desktop). The coordinates appear — tap them to copy. On desktop you can also just copy the whole browser URL.
  3. Paste below. One shop per line. Add a name before the coordinates if you want: Sharma Chicken, 21.1458, 79.0882
Plain coordinates, "Name, lat, lon" lines, and Google Maps links all work.
Set your location first — GPS, a place name, or a tap on the map.
